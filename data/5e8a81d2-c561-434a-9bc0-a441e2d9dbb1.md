Furthermore, the interactive nature of the "guessing game" serves as a bridge between classmates. As we piece together the clues to uncover the identity of a peer, we are essentially exploring the diverse facets of their personality. This mutual recognition can effectively bridge the gap between students, transforming superficial acquaintances into meaningful friendships.

I firmly believe that such a column will evolve into a platform for emotional connection, adding both intellectual and sentimental value to our newspaper.
