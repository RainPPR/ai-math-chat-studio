### 构思拆解
- **任务本质**：一封给外教的建议邮件，必须明确选择“Guess who I am”，并给出理由。
- **立意锚点**：  
  1. **自我认知**——同学们在描述自己供他人猜测时，被迫思考“我是谁”，从而促进自我觉察与身份认同。  
  2. **同伴认知与关系增进**——读者根据线索猜测作者身份的过程，天然成为同学们互相了解彼此特质、故事的互动，从而拉近班级关系。
- **语篇结构**：问候并亮明选择 → 理由一层（自我认知） → 理由二层（同学关系） → 收尾致谢与期待。

### 范文呈现
Dear Jenny,

I am writing to share my preference for the new column of our class English newspaper. Among the two proposals you offered, I firmly choose “Guess who I am”.

The primary reason is that this column acts as a mirror for self-reflection. When students write anonymous descriptions of their own personalities and experiences, they are gently led to ponder the question “Who am I”. Such self-exploration cultivates a clearer sense of identity and helps us recognize our true strengths. Besides, the guessing game bridges the gap between contributors and readers. As classmates read the clues and discuss who the writer might be, they naturally learn about each other’s quirks and stories, which strengthens the bond within the whole class.

I truly appreciate your creative ideas, and I am convinced this column will make our newspaper more engaging and our class more united.

Yours,  
Li Hua

### 教师点评
1. **词汇把控**：使用了 `proposals`, `firmly`, `self-reflection`, `ponder`, `cultivate`, `identity`, `bridge the gap`, `quirks`, `bond`, `engaging` 等高考高频核心词。这些词精准且地道，既避开了过于简单的表达（如把 think 升级为 ponder，把 good 升级为 engaging），也没有硬塞超纲生僻词，完全落在学生可掌控的范围内。
2. **句式设计**：开篇用介词短语交待背景；理由段交替使用了表语从句（`that this column acts as...`）、时间状语从句（`When students write...`）、定语从句（`which strengthens...`）以及 `as` 引导的从句，连续句子结构不同，逻辑推进自然。
3. **立意闭环**：从“描述自己→反思自我”到“猜测互动→了解同伴→凝聚班级”，严格遵循了你提出的“自我认知、朋友同学认知”核心立意，情感升华不空洞，首尾呼应外教的建议。
