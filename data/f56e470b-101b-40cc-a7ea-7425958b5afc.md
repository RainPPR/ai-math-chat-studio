> "Food is so fundamental to life and good health and it is absolutely worthy of some time in the basic curriculum," says the USDA's Stacy Dean.

1. 食物对生命和健康至关重要（fundamental to life and good health）；
2. 食物相关教育值得在基础课程中占据一定时间（worthy of some time in the basic curriculum）。
